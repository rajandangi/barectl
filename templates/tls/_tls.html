TLS plans

Renewal setup installs the distribution's Certbot and guards its scheduled renewal; a challenge route lets an existing site answer HTTP-01 challenges from its own webroot. The readiness review reads fresh DNS, the server's addresses, its clock and the authority directory's reachability. Preparing any of them only reads the server, as root or through noninteractive sudo, and reviews every change. Neither renewal setup nor a challenge route orders a certificate or contacts an authority; a staging order does, only when it is applied.
