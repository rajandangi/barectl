# Review destructive restore and coordinated capture

Accepted for planned v0.6. A separately permitted restore may replace the selected site's application data and bound database after exact artifact validation, a reviewed safety capture, persistent gating and positive writer draining. This is a narrow destructive-recovery exception to the create/finish and preserve-data behavior in [ADR 0009](0009-review-native-file-changes-before-site-mutation.md) and [ADR 0015](0015-recognize-only-the-convention.md), not permission to replace resources in other workflows or adopt foreign configuration.

Files and database do not form one atomic transaction. Full capture and restore require the supported exclusive-maintenance contract, disclose shared FPM effects, preserve target native identities/grants, and leave partial or unknown recovery gated with background boot activation inhibited. Live archive/dump combinations, automatic rollback and blind retry were rejected because they cannot establish a coherent recovery point or prove which destructive effects occurred. [Native recovery rules](../backups-native-design.md) govern supported mechanics and ordinary-administration repair; app or administrator work outside cooperation is not fenced by the lock.
